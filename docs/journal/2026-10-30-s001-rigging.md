# Session 001: Rigging

## Intent

The Steward opened the project with a brief, written in Russian, that set out: British English for everything in the repository, conversation in the interlocutor's language, untranslated terms with a glossary; the use of existing works within licence terms or for study only; real sound on macOS and in the browser; sessions of different depth; an interest in light dynamics, dynamic AI interfaces, the Model as frontman, the human as Director or Follower, VR and 3D; atmospheric electronic composition with a dynamic, saturated narrative as the base style; publication on GitHub under a CC licence with attribution; exact provenance (model, time, roles) everywhere. The ask for this session: prepare the standard rig so that the first step can be taken.

## What happened

The Model built the rig in one pass:

- Documents: `README.md`, `LICENCE` (CC BY 4.0), `CLAUDE.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, `PROVENANCE.md`, `CITATION.cff`.
- Research framing: `docs/research-programme.md`, `docs/session-levels.md`, this journal and its template.
- macOS instrument: `tools/macos/render.py`, additive synthesis in pure Python to a WAV file, playback through `afplay`, with a harmonic report of every partial.
- Browser instrument: `tools/browser/`, the Harmonic Playground on the Web Audio API: eight partials over one fundamental, presets, a slow "breathe" drift, a convolution space, a spectrum and waveform scope, and the page's light coupled to pitch class and loudness.
- `Makefile` targets, `.gitignore`, a browser preview configuration, memory notes for the Model.

## What we heard

A smoke test only: A2 (110 Hz) with five harmonics at 1/n amplitudes through `afplay`. Results are under "Tests" below.

## What we derived

Nothing yet; this was infrastructure. The harmonic report already shows one seed for the next session: partial 7 of any fundamental sits 31 cents below the nearest equal-tempered note, partial 11 sits 49 cents off.

## Tests

- `tools/macos/render.py`: A2 (110 Hz) with five harmonics at 1/n, two seconds, rendered in 0.09 s in pure Python (NumPy absent on the Steward's machine) and played through `afplay`, exit code 0. `afinfo` on the result: WAVE, 1 channel, 48 000 Hz, Int16, 2.000 s. A just-intonation triad over 110 Hz (`--ratios 1,5/4,3/2`) rendered as well. The report named partial 5 as C#5 at -14 cents, the first place where the series and equal temperament visibly part.
- `tools/browser/`: served at `http://localhost:8765` from `.claude/launch.json`; the page loaded with no console messages. Pressing Start created an AudioContext at 48 000 Hz in state `running`; the eight partial gains read 1, 0.5, 0.333, 0.25, 0.2, 0.167, 0.143, 0.125 with the sum normalised to 0.368; the scope showed eight peaks on the log-frequency axis and the sawtooth-like waveform expected from a 1/n series; the page tinted to the hue of pitch class A. Stop paused the context.
- `make check` passes (Python byte-compile and `node --check`); `make help` lists the targets.

## Decisions

- The human partner is called **the Steward**. The brief's word «стюарт» was read as "steward", the custodian who sets direction; to be confirmed by the Steward.
- The attribution name "Leonid" is provisional until the Steward confirms the public name and handle.
- The working title "Vestacore Music" follows the repository path.
- One licence, CC BY 4.0, for everything including code, with the caveat recorded in `LICENCE`.
- The Steward's pre-existing empty folder `session-techno-ui/` sets the convention: session material lives in root-level `session-<name>/` folders.

## Candidate first steps

1. **Hear the harmonic series (L3 → L1).** Derive partials 1 to 16 of A1 (55 Hz), render them one by one and stacked with `render.py`, listen, and note where the ear disagrees with the arithmetic (partials 7, 11 and 13 against equal temperament).
2. **Breathing drone (L0, Director).** In the Harmonic Playground the Steward gives emotional and imagistic feedback while the Model shapes partials, space and drift; the words are recorded beside the parameters as the first feedback-mapping data.
3. **First sketch with a plot (L2).** A sixty-second atmospheric opening in three states (stillness, swell, release), built from additive pads rendered by `render.py` and assembled in sequence.
